# Greetings

Every word in this lesson becomes two flashcards: Korean → English and English → Korean. Its example sentences are used as cloze cards, with the word blanked out. Press **Study** on the lesson list to start.

## Saying hello

<TTS>안녕하세요</TTS> is polite and safe with anyone. Tap the speaker to hear it.

| Korean | When |
| --- | --- |
| 안녕하세요 | Hello (polite) |
| 안녕 | Hi (to friends) |

## Introducing yourself

Say your name with **저는 … 이에요/예요**: 이에요 after a consonant, 예요 after a vowel.

<details>
<summary>Examples</summary>

- 저는 **학생**이에요. (학생 ends in a consonant)
- 저는 민수**예요**. (민수 ends in a vowel)

</details>
